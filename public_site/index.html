About

I'm a product designer. I sit with real users, map their workflows, and turn what I learn into research-driven UX/UI and design systems. AI — Claude, Cursor, Higgsfield — is part of how I work, taking me from problem to prototype faster. And as an extra edge, I can build what I design in React and Tailwind, so the intent survives to production. Grounded in human-centred design (ISO 9241-210).
