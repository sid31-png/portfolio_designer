Color

Navy is the brand (structure, headings, ink). Magenta is action (CTAs, active states, key links) — used sparingly. Neutrals are cool, low-chroma and tinted toward navy, never pure gray. Semantic colors are muted jewel tones, not vitamin-label brights.
